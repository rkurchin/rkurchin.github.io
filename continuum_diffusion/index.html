Cooling Rate & Coring: When cooling is slow (< 0.1 K/s), solid back-diffusion DS has ample time to homogenize the grain → Equilibrium Solidification (Lever Rule, uniform solid). As cooling accelerates (> 5 K/s), solid diffusion freezes (DS ≈ 0) → Scheil Coring (radial gradient from pure core kC₀ to solute-rich rim), shifting average solid C̄S leftward.
